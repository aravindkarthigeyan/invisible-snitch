## 1. Method (all exact; verified to ≥ 20 digits against ODE integration and finite differences)

For a straight run, with τ := tan(β/2): C = ρg(β) conserved, y = Cτ^v, ρcos β = (C/2)(τ^{v−1} − τ^{v+1}), h = (C/2)((1+v)τ^{v−1} − (1−v)τ^{1+v}),
h′ = −(1−v²) (Lemma 1). New tools:
* **Lemma 2 (tangent transport).** Ξ := x_θ + v e^{iβ} conj(x_θ) = ∂_θh + i τ^v ∂_θC: Re Ξ and Im Ξ/τ^v are constants of the motion per label.
  So the tangent after any straight run is explicit: tan ψ = [(1+v cos β)Y_r − v sin β]/[1 − v cos β − v sin β Y_r], Y_r = y ∂_θlog C/∂_θh.
* **Lemma 3 (curvature transport).** J := ∂_θh ∂²_θC − ∂_θC ∂²_θh is conserved and
  k = τ^vJ/((1−v²)w³) − (v sin φ/((1−v²)ρ))[ρ ∂_θh ∂_θlog C/w² − v − cos(φ−ψ)]. Between two times: k″ = (τ″/τ′)^v(w′/w″)³(k′ + 𝔖′) − 𝔖″.
These capture the cancellations that the differential inequalities of [B]/P7 lose (e.g. the O(v) tilt transient when a candidate is
passed abeam is exactly reversible). Everything else is bookkeeping: run 1 from the unit circle (J = (1−v²)tan^{−v}(α/2)); exact tangency
