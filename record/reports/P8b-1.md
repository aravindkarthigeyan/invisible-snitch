## 0. Result in one paragraph

**PROVED (referee/P8b-claims.md, Theorem P8.M, Corollary P8.X): the main theorem and the exact-capture corollary hold
for an explicit path ("design T_g"), with explicit absolute constants v₀ > 0, T₀, CONDITIONAL on exactly two inputs:
[P7] (Theorem P7.B, Corollary P7.E — passed by RP7-2) and the touch-down hypothesis (H_T) (six pointwise/pairwise
statements about the exact state at touch-down; stream P8a).** The main theorem is therefore NOT yet unconditional:
(H_T) is P8a's job, and two of its items — (X1), (X2) in §2 — are not in P8a's interim statement. Everything between
"touch-down data" and "every direction is seen by time T₀" is written out; no other hypothesis is left.

The assembly is much shorter than the brief anticipated, because of three design/proof decisions:

1. **No hole.** The hole labels |α| < α_s are *seen but still exist*: the true candidates there keep moving by the
   true law and form a C^{1,1} curve (P8a S1: curvature ≤ 1.14 up to the axis; N8: a parabola). Only the *virtual*
   candidates of [B] are rough there (they went through the cut-off disc). So Construction C is run on a **working
   front** = true front through the hole ("ghost labels": nothing has to be seen there). Lemma P8.W: every lemma of [B]
   and Theorem P7.B/Cor. P7.E hold for a working front (their proofs use only the ODE x′ = vF(x − P) and the
   variational equation). No cut, no connector, no restart at the far edge, no "gap lemma".
2. **No modification of P7.B and no passive phase for Γ.** At touch-down P7.B's separation (S1) fails only for pairs
   (a near X₀, b near E_Y). Instead of extending P7.B's far-pair estimate, the sweep is cut in time: a short
   *departure* [t₂, t_I] (base within W of X₀; W an explicit constant) and then Theorem P7.B **verbatim** from the leg
   start t_I on the whole remaining arc (through the hole, into E_Y). At t_I Harry is at distance ≥ W/2 from the tail,
   so (S1) holds; the tail is then an ordinary far part of P7.B's arc, and the last legs into E_Y are P7.B(b). What has
   to be proved is only (ST_end) at t_I (Proposition P8.I).
3. **Pairwise lemmas ⇒ no straddling pairs.** (ST_end) at t_I is obtained pair by pair from three overlapping
   regimes: (A) pairs in the stage-A arc next to the base: invariants of P7.B (first application, from t₂, on the
   forward arc only, where (S1) is fine); (D) pairs in the tail {u′ ≤ W}: new departure theorem P8.D; (P) all other
   pairs: passive lemma, stated for the hull of the *pair* (Lemma P8.A). The regimes overlap by more than the largest
   scale Λ_I considered, so every pair lies entirely in one of them.

Exponent/restart budget: (H_T) (a′, γ_T) → γ₁ = min(a′/2, 1/4) (Cor. P7.E at t₂, forward arc) → departure at
γ_* = min(a′/2, γ_T) with loss 1.39v → a_I = γ_*/2 → γ_B = γ_*/4 (Cor. P7.E at t_I). **Two applications of Cor. P7.E,
one restart**; P7.D, P7.H, P7.T and the restart Remark of P7 §2 are not used.

Answers to the five points of the brief:

1. **Departure from E_Y.** The geometry is that of P7.D after reflection (û := −n̂, Harry recedes from the tail along
   its line), with three differences, all handled in Theorem P8.D: Harry starts at X₀, i.e. at distance
   z₀ ≈ ε^{1/(2−2v)} *already departed* (this only helps: every estimate holds with u replaced by u + z₀), with lateral
   offset y₀ (needed: |y₀| ≤ ϑ(z₀+ε)(ε/2)^γ; true: y₀ ≈ ε^{1−v/2} ≪ ε^{3/4}); and the path is the leg polygon. "Straight
   back" is replaced by (DP): unit speed, heading within ϑ_h(τ+ε)^γ of n̂. This is supplied by Lemma P8.H from the
   forward invariants (tilt lemma) of a first application of P7.B on the forward arc. **The Hölder decay of the
   heading deviation at τ → 0 is essential**: it turns the rigid rotation of the tail into the uniformly small term
   (4.2v/γ)ϑ_h a^γ; a constant deviation ω would give ω·log(1/ε)-type terms — N8's "coarse first chord ⇒ transient
   hook" and (F1). I work with POINTWISE tilt data (available from formulas) instead of P7.D's L² data (D1): with L²
   data the same argument leaves an ε-dependent factor (τ/ε)^{O(v)} on the rotation term (stretch w̃ only bounded by
   𝒜), which I could not remove; pointwise it is clean. P7.T's restrictions disappear (P7.T is not used).
   *P7.B's separation:* (S1)/(CA) is used through (∗) ρ_s ≥ ½min(w_s,32R) − 2ε of Step 1(d); for s in the tail and
   the base near X₀ this is false. Rather than extend the far-pair estimate (which would also need the two-scale
   argument below), P7.B is restarted at t_I. *Two scales are unavoidable in this proof:* heading control needs
   W ≤ 10⁻⁴R_A (tilt-lemma constant 0.33(λ/R)^γ), while separation at the restart needs R_B ≤ W/36.
2. **Passive phase and hand-over.** Not needed for the long sweep (point 2 above). The only passive stretch is
   [t₂,t_I] for pairs away from both X₀ and E_Y (Lemma P8.A, ρ₀ = W/5, additive term (132v/W)λ^{3/2}). Straddling
   pairs: none, by overlap of the three pairwise regimes (claims §6).
3. **The hole.** Removed (point 1). Seen labels need no attention (Lemma P8.W(c): only true labels' directions are
   claimed seen); unseen labels are not skipped because C runs through with ε-steps of arclength of the working
   front (hole labels are simply part of it). What is needed from P8a is (X2) below.
4. **Last legs, "everything is seen", time, Lipschitz.** P7.B(b) at the second application; true candidates via
   Lemma P8.W(b),(c). T_stop ≤ t₂ + 2.1W + 2U_I/(1−v), U_I ≤ (9/8)U. The path is T1 + T2 + legs, each leg computed
   from the past, piecewise linear, speed ≤ 1, continuous at all junctions (no connector).
5. **Main theorem with constants:** claims §5–§6. With P8a's interim constants (a′ = 1/2, C₀ = 0.86, λ₀ = 1/40,
   c_sep = 0.024, γ_T = 1/4, ϑ_T ≈ 4.3 after conversion, U_max = 6.4): R_A ≈ 5·10⁻⁷, ϑ_h ≈ 18, W ≈ 1.4·10⁻¹¹, R_B ≈ 8·10⁻⁴³,
   **v₀ ≈ 1.6·10⁻⁵¹**, T₀ ≈ 3.74 + 15.7 ≈ 19.5; ε_* ≈ 10⁻⁴⁴ (larger ε use the ε_* path). Wasteful but positive and
   absolute. (True times are ≈ 9–15, §3.)

## 5. What is NOT done / caveats

* **(H_T) is a hypothesis.** Items (X1), (X2) must be added to P8a's list (§2). If P8a's final constants or the
  definition of t_s change, only claims §5 changes.
* Lemma P8.W(d) asserts that the proofs of P7.B/P7.E go through for a working front; I checked the list of citations,
  not every line again. A referee of P7 can confirm in minutes (the proofs never use x_θ(0) = e^{iθ} beyond
  |∂_θx(0)| = 1). If someone objects to ghost labels on principle: the same assembly works with the cut +
  connector design, but then a genuine "arrival across a gap" lemma is needed (frozen-direction estimates are useless
  for arrivals — the pointwise tilt against a fixed direction is truly amplified by (ρ₀/ρ)^μ on approach; one needs a
  pair bootstrap across the gap). I did not prove that; the ghost front makes it unnecessary.
* Theorem P8.D needs POINTWISE tilt data at t₂ (T3), (T4) — natural for formula data, but not available from P7.B's
  invariants; so P8.D does not replace P7.D in a two-pass design.
* v₀ ≈ 10⁻⁵¹ is an artefact of chaining constants (R ∝ (γ/400C)^{1/a′} twice, W ∝ 10^{−1/γ}R_A). Cheap gains: the
  referee's γ = a′ − 22v form of Cor. P7.E; a tilt lemma with the start tilt instead of 0.33(λ/R)^γ.
* K̃-type bounds (Theorem R) are not produced for the departure stretch and are not needed (time bound from P7.B(b)).
