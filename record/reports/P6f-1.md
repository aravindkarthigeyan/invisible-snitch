## 3. Persistence: where the difficulty really is

**3.1 What is needed is very weak (PROVED, Lemma F2 / Theorem F3).** d/dt |x_{b_e} − P| = v f(D) − u·r̂_E. So if the far
end stays within the angle arccos(c_0) of the heading, the stretch lasts ≤ D/(c_0 − v). Under (LL_{1/4}) on scales
≥ σ_0 and base at ≥ 13σ_0: heading within 0.2607 of the backward tangent, far end within 0.4377, so u·r̂_E ≥ 0.765.
NUMERICAL (model hook, inward walk, base in the inner half of the arm, R > 16ε; max over ε = 10⁻³ … 10⁻⁵, and 10⁻⁶ for
v = 0.2, 0.3): far-end bearing ≤ 0.007 / 0.046 / 0.20 / 0.24 / 0.29 rad for v = 0.05 / 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3 / 0.4; hull-perimeter
rate ≤ −1.9 / −1.8 / −1.57 / −1.36 / −1.14; sector opening B at Harry ≤ 0.29.

**3.2 Why (E_Q) at entry cannot be enough (HEURISTIC; exact ingredients).** On a straight run the ordinate of a candidate
relative to Harry's line is y = C tan^v(β/2), and h = ρ(cos β + v) decreases at the rate 1 − v² for every candidate
(P6c §0). For small bearings this gives y ∝ ρ^{−μ} at fixed along-track separations: a tilt ω of a short piece of front
against the line of sight obeys tan ω(t) = tan ω(0)·(ρ(0)/ρ(t))^μ (A-4's kink amplification, P6c's hook law).
Consequences for a front that Harry approaches along its own axis from distance L:
 * a smooth bend of angle θ_0 at distance r_0 from the far end, spread over a length ~r_0 (so Q ≈ θ_0 there), has, when
   Harry is at distance ρ ≥ r_0 from it, the angle arctan(θ_0 (L/ρ)^μ), and the turning concentrates where the local
   tilt passes through (ρ/L)^μ, i.e. on a length ≈ r_0 (ρ/L)^μ/θ_0. For r_0 ≪ L θ_0^{1/μ} this is a corner of angle ≈ π/2
   with radius ≪ r_0 at distance r_0 from the end: |Q| ≈ θ_0 (L/r_0)^μ ≫ 1 there. So the class {|Q| ≤ Q̄} is not invariant,
   with no bound on the loss, for general data once ε ≪ L Q̄^{1/μ}.
 * saturation is in the ANGLE, not in Q: tilts never cross π/2 (P6c Claim 12), so corners tend to right angles.
 * an exact power-law profile is stable: tan ω = c σ^{−a} is mapped to c' σ^{−a−μ}, with |Q| ≤ (a + μ)/2. The model hook
   is of this type (a = v/(1+v)), and its perturbations (arm curvature, Q_0 = O(σ)) are amplified by at most σ^{−2μ}, which
   is harmless for a remainder O(σ^a), a > 2μ. This is why the real arm survives (numerics) while the class does not.
 * CONJECTURE (not accessible numerically; needs ε ≪ e^{−c/v²}): for the true arm the same computation predicts a second
   corner (after Harry has turned through the first hook his line of sight is rotated by ≈ π/2 and the inner profile
   is again infinitely tilted at the end), and so on — a polygonal inward spiral with right-angle corners at
   super-exponentially separated scales. Then (P) with q = 1/4 fails near the corners and (H_{c_0}) fails within the
   relative distance ≈ c_0 of a corner, while the hull lemma with γ ≈ π/2 would still hold.

## 4. The convexity route (Lead's (3)): negative (HEURISTIC, from the exact law)

With P on the front, Lemma 1(iv) of P6a reads, to leading order in φ, κ_t(s) = (v/ρ)(κ(s) − κ̄(s)),
κ̄ := ρ^{−2}∫_0^s κ d(ρ²). (For constant κ the exact right side is −(3v/2ρ) sin²φ·κ.) The non-local term opposes κ(s)
wherever |κ(s)| < |κ̄(s)|: a one-signed curvature is not preserved; an inflection is created where the curvature dips
below its ρ²-weighted mean over the arc between Harry and the point. (Example: for a fixed Harry a straight segment not
through Harry becomes an arc of a conchoid of Nicomedes, which has inflections.) On a hook (|κ| ≈ c/σ, decreasing
outward):
 * walking inward, |κ(s)| > |κ̄|: curvature is amplified at the rate v/ρ (the sharpening of §3.2), sign kept;
 * walking outward, |κ(s)| < |κ̄|, but κ(s) − κ̄ ≈ −cρ/(3σ²), so κ_t ≈ −vc/(3σ²) for a time ≈ σ: a relative change O(v), once.
So there is no maximum principle, and "the remainder lies in the half-plane of the tangent at Harry" is not available
as an invariant (also: an arc of one-signed curvature and total turning > π need not lie on one side of its tangent).

## 5. Proposal: the reversed final stretch (HEURISTIC + NUMERICAL; nothing proved)

**Rule.** At the entry leg start t_{j_1}: (R1) leave the front and run, far from the arm, to a staging point
Z = x_{b_e} − D_0 d, where d is a fixed unit vector making an angle ≥ π/6 with every tangent of the arm (e.g. d at 45° to
the axis of the arm, on the side towards which the hook turns); (R2) run straight at the virtual candidate x_{b_e}
until it is met; (R3) apply the leg rule of C on the arc [a_{j_1}, b_e] with the labels in DECREASING order, base b_e.
The path is fixed in advance and 1-Lipschitz; seeing is Lemma C(3) verbatim.

**Why it should be lossless.**
 * Exposure. A tangent rotates at the rate (v/2ρ)|sin 2φ| ≤ v/(2ρ) and lengths stretch at the rate v sin²φ/ρ. Outward
   walk, point at distance r from E, Harry at σ ≤ r/2: ρ ≳ r/2 for a time ≈ r/2, so ∫ v dt/ρ = O(v), ONCE. Inward walk,
   Harry between the distances 2r and L from E: ∫ v dt/ρ ≈ v log(L/2r) — the per-scale loss. In the near field
   (σ ∈ [r/2, r]) the line-of-sight tilt of the point is ≈ q(r − σ)/(2r) under (LL_q), so the rotation is
   ≈ ∫ (v/ρ)·qρ/(2r) dσ ≈ vq/4: again O(vq), once. In the variable log σ the linearised evolution of Q is a Volterra
   equation with a kernel of total mass O(v): sup|Q| ≤ Q̄/(1 − Cv). (The near-field statement needs a propagated modulus
   of continuity, as in Theorem B; that is the open item (R-b).)
 * The approach (R2) is a straight run, hence exactly computable: tilts against d obey tan ω → K tan ω, K = (D_0/ρ)^μ ≥ 1,
   so Q' = [K/(cos²ω + K² sin²ω)]·Q + μ sin ω cos ω·K/(cos²ω + K² sin²ω), and
   sup_{K ≥ 1} K/(cos²ω + K² sin²ω) = 1/sin(2 min(ω, π/4)): for tangents at angle ≥ π/6 from d, |Q'| ≤ 1.16(|Q| + μ/2),
   independently of K, i.e. of ε. Approaching from the side "combs" the arm; approaching along its axis is the unstable
   direction ω = 0 (factor K). This is P6c's invariant Z = Q/sin 2φ (Claim 14(c)) with the sign condition (SG) enforced
   by design.
 * The last points seen are those next to the entry label: a regular piece of the bulk front that Harry left sideways.

**Open sub-lemmas (precise gaps).**
 (R-a) *Comb lemma*: (E_Q*) + tangent cone at entry ⇒ (E_Q*) with constant ≈ 1.16(Q̄ + μ/2) at the end of (R2), uniformly
       in ε. Needs the exact transport of tangents on a straight run (P6c Claim 3) including the bearing spread of the
       arm seen from Harry near the end of the run, and the staging leg (R1) (far field, Gronwall). I expect this to be
       provable with the existing tools; it needs a tangent cone of opening < 2π/3 for the arm at entry.
 (R-b) *Outward bootstrap* ("Theorem B″"): the invariants of Theorem B in the scale-invariant class E(a,b) ≤ κ²λ³/σ²
       (λ ≤ σ/8) on the arc AHEAD of an outward-walking base at distance σ from E. By the exposure count there is no
       per-scale loss, so a fixed-constant class should be invariant — unlike the inward Theorem B′. Theorem B cannot be
       used as a black box: its output (I2) is weaker than its input (IC1), so it cannot be restarted at each scale,
       and v(4U + 2) ≤ 10⁻⁶R limits one application to a scale ratio ~10⁻⁷/v. This is the main open item.
 (R-c) *Regular end*: the piece next to the entry label, left sideways at t_{j_1}, satisfies (ST) when Harry returns.

NUMERICAL (model; `runs.out`, `deep.out`): on the outward walk sup|Q| is flat in ε (tables in §3.2); far-end bearing

## 6. Numerics: which quantity is monotone or bounded (model; NUMERICAL)

`fs.py v eps in|out [L0 wiggle rows]`: graded adaptive grid (spacing max(ε/4, 0.02·min(ρ, distance to the ends))), RK4,
3 substeps per leg, C¹ virtual field of P6a. Per leg: R, sector opening B at Harry (containing the heading), far-end
bearing, K̃, tangent span, sup|Q|, number of sign changes of the bearing increment, hull perimeter Π.
 * Hull-perimeter rate: ≤ −1.0 in all hook runs; never close to 0. Sector opening B ≤ 0.29 (inward, inner half),
   ≤ 0.64 (outward). K̃ ≤ 0.12 in all hook runs.
 * Bearing order: not monotone (0–20 sign changes of the bearing increment). Curvature sign: not tested separately
   (§4 shows it is not preserved).
 * Tangent span: grows with log(1/ε) (inward, v = 0.2: 0.34 → 0.60 → 0.81 → 0.95 rad for ε = 10⁻³ → 10⁻⁶): not ε-uniform
   at accessible ε, consistent with a hook that saturates at π/2.
 * sup|Q|: bounded but ε-dependent inward (growing for wiggled data); flat outward (§3.2).
Double precision is not an issue here (coordinates centred at the far end; ε ≥ 10⁻⁶ against positions O(0.1)).
The simulations are of a MODEL of the final stretch, not of start S + C_cut; a run of the full construction at
ε ≤ 10⁻⁵ was not attempted.
