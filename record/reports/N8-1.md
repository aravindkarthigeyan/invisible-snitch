## 2. Design T: definition (precise) and exact facts

Parameters: v, ε, D₁ > 0 (used: D₁ = 0.1). g(b) = sin b / tan^v(b/2), g_max = g(arccos v).
* (T1) P(t) = (t, 0), 0 ≤ t ≤ t₁ := 1/(1−v) + D₁. Front for α ∈ (0, π), polar (ρ, β) about P(t):
  ρ g(β) = g(α), ρ(cos β + v) = cos α + v − (1−v²)t (mirror for the other half). Seen: |α| ≤ α_s, g(α_s) = ε g_max.
* (T2) α₀ := arccos(v + 1/t₁) (the unique zero of ∂β/∂α: (1−v²)t₁(cos α − v) = 1 − v²). P runs at unit speed along the
  ray from P(t₁) through x_{α₀}(t₁) until it meets x_{α₀}, at t₂ = t₁ + ρ₀/(1−v), ρ₀ = ρ(α₀, t₁) (chase lengths 0.60, 0.52,
  0.48 for v = 0.2, 0.1, 0.05). With (r, b) the polar coordinates of a candidate about P(t₁) relative to this ray,
  C₂ = r sin b / tan^v(b/2) and h₂ = r(cos b + v) − (1−v²)(t − t₁) give the state at t₂ in closed form. b ≥ 0 has a double
  zero at α₀ (both neighbours on the same side of the ray): tangency. Seen during (T2): the labels α ∈ (b_e, α₀] with
  C₂(α) ≤ ε g_max (they pass abeam at distance C₂/g_max); b_e := the solution of C₂ = ε g_max below α₀.
* (T3) Construction C with base x_{α₀} at t₂, labels increasing; cut at the hole (after the leg ending at 2π − α_s one
  leg straight at x_{α_s}); stop when the remaining arc up to b_e (+2π) has length ≤ ε.
All candidates are then seen: |α| ≤ α_s in (T1), (b_e, α₀] in (T2), the rest by C.

Exact local exponents at touch-down (derivation: C₂ ≈ ρ₀^v (κ s²/2)^{1−v} 2^v for a candidate at distance s from the
tangency point on a front with relative curvature κ; then the two integrals):
 forward arc: bearing from the heading ∝ s^{(1−2v)/(1−v)} (measured exponent 0.7500 at v = 0.2 for 10⁻⁹ ≤ s ≤ 10⁻³);
 behind: lateral offset ∝ s^{(2−v)/(1+v)}, tilt ∝ s^{(1−2v)/(1+v)} (measured 0.500 at v = 0.2, 0.727 at v = 0.1);
 |E_Y − X₀| ∝ ε^{1/(2−2v)}; hole width ∝ ε^{1/(1+v)}.
