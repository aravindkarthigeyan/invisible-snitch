## 3. The rotating-strain heuristic: what is true (Task 3)

Lead's model: in the frame of the line of sight ℓ (Harry → E) the inner arm is strained transversally at the rate μ
per e-fold while ℓ rotates at the rate q: M' = [[0, q], [−q, μ]] M; bounded eccentricity iff q > μ/2.
**Measured: the linear model is exactly right, the eccentricity is NOT bounded, and it does not matter.**
 * Strain per e-fold of D: d ln det M/d ln(1/D) = v/(cos b − v) =: μ_eff (exact: dD/dt = v − cos b, b = bearing of E from
   the heading; measured 0.4318, 0.2505, 0.1111 for v = 0.3, 0.2, 0.1).
 * Winding per e-fold: q* = 0.1465, 0.0723, 0.0236 — below μ_eff/2 = 0.216, 0.125, 0.056, as the Lead feared.
 * Eccentricity of M grows like D^{−0.317}, D^{−0.2045}, D^{−0.1006} = D^{−μ_eff cos 2θ_E}: unbounded.
 * **But the arm is not generic data.** At the entry its inner part is X(σ) = σ e_∞ + kσ^{1+a'} e_⊥ + …, with a' = v
   (measured local exponent of |ψ(σ) − ψ(0)| at the entry, v = 0.2: 0.2028, 0.2018, 0.2008, 0.2003, 0.2002, 0.2001 at
   σ = 10⁻⁷, 10⁻⁹, …, 10⁻¹⁷; it is the straight-run law: tilt ∝ r^{v/(1+v)}, arclength ∝ r^{1/(1+v)}). Later the inner arm is
   M·X. The deviation from straightness decays like σ^{v} while the eccentricity grows, and the two balance at a
   FIXED POINT of the scale-to-scale map. HEURISTIC derivation: the remaining arc at scale D is u A₁ + u^{1+v} A₂ + …,
   A₁ = σ_L M e_∞, A₂ = kσ_L^{1+v} M e_⊥; stationarity of |A₁|/D and |A₂|/D, with d ln|M e_∞| = μ_eff sin²θ_E d ln(1/D)
   (exact) and det M ∝ D^{−μ_eff}, gives

   **sin²θ_E = (μ_eff − v) / (μ_eff (2 + v)),   q* = μ_eff sin θ_E cos θ_E,   μ_eff = v/(cos b* − v).**

   | v | sin²θ_E measured | predicted | q* measured | predicted |

   (v = 0.05, 0.02: sin²θ_E = 0.030, 0.018 and still falling towards 0.0244, 0.0099 at the deepest scales.)
   For small v: θ_E ≈ (v/2)^{1/2}, q* ≈ v^{3/2}/√2 — the Lead's "v^{3/2}", but as the winding rate of a stationary pattern,
   not as a growth exponent. In the Lead's matrix the eigenvalues are μ sin²θ_E (recessive; its eigenvector is the
   tangent at E) and μ cos²θ_E; REAL eigenvalues (q < μ/2) are what a stationary pattern needs.
 * **The true condition** is therefore not q > μ/2 but a' > 0: the deviation of the last-met end from a straight line
   must decay like a positive power a' of the distance. Then sin²θ = (μ − a')/(μ(2 + a')) for a' < μ and θ = 0 (the

   round-off) the formula gives θ = π/4, q = μ/2 — exactly the Lead's marginal case — and there is no bounded regime:
   this is P6f's ε^{−2μ}-growth and the double-precision breakdown of §1.5.
 * Stability: after the peak the bearing relaxes like a small power of D (≈ D^{2(μ−v)}; 0.1 per e-fold at v = 0.2,
   ≈ 2v² for small v); no growing mode was seen in any run, including the multiprecision ones.
 * Universality: the fixed point depends only on v — an arc of curvature 1 left at a wedge angle 0.02, 2 arcsin v or
   π/2 (`alt.py gen`) reaches the same b* = 0.0572 (v = 0.2); only the transient differs (for a perpendicular
   departure there is no overshoot at all: the bearing rises monotonically to b*).

## 4. Alternatives (Task 4) — not needed, tested anyway

Since the endgame does not degrade, no redesign is required. For completeness, at the same depths:

**(b) "Eat the left arm first, then turn around" = tangential departure from the last-met end.** Model
(`alt.py gen`, `out/generic_end_omega.png`): an arc of curvature 1 whose end T Harry leaves straight along the
backward tangent (wedge ω = 0), far detour, return, inward walk with rule C. The last-met end is then the turning
point; it has NO hook: the straight-run law gives lateral offsets ∝ r^{2−v/(1+v)}, i.e. a' = 1/(1+v) > μ (for
v < √2 − 1), so θ* = 0: the remainder straightens and the far-end bearing decays like D^{1/(1+v) − μ}.
Measured (ε = 10⁻³⁶): bearing 0.0208, 0.0037, 0.0008, 0.0002, … per decade for v = 0.2 (fitted exponent 0.59, predicted
0.583), exponent 0.83 for v = 0.1 (predicted 0.798), down to 10⁻¹⁰, with Q, B, K̃ → 0 likewise; T/L = 1/(1−v).
(Below 10⁻¹⁰ the double run shows its own round-off growing like D^{−μ}: the straight state is as unstable to
non-decaying noise as everything else.) Caveats for a proof: the turning point must be at a fixed distance D from
the corner (below scale D the departure is tangential; above it the wedge 2 arcsin v is seen again, harmlessly), and
the RIGHT arm then starts from a hooked end (a slow turn |Q| ≲ v/2 on scales ≤ D/λ_c), which is outside the proved
start condition (ST). So (b) trades a benign endgame for a new start problem. Not recommended unless the proof of the
spiral endgame fails.

**(c) Other designs** (HEURISTIC, from §3): what decides the endgame is only the exponent a' of the last-met end.
Any end left at a non-zero angle has a' = v and the universal spiral; an end left tangentially has a' = 1/(1+v) and
no spiral; alternating between two ends, or a transversal final approach, cannot change a' and was not simulated.
