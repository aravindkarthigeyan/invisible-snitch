# F2-1: the v = 0.8 breakdown in extended precision, and the fixed-v, eps -> 0 leg-greedy chase

Everything in this report is **NUMERICAL**. Code: `code/F2/` (it reuses the exact straight-step kernel of
`code/F/snitch_core.py` and the simulator `code/F/sim.py`). Logs: `code/F2/logs/`, figures and data: `code/F2/out/`.
d = 1, Harry speed 1, eps = vision radius.

## Summary
* **Task 1 (v = 0.8, two-point controller k = -1, c = 2, eps = 0.1): classification (a), artefact.** The breakdown at
  t ~ 150 is a double-precision artefact. With the same recorded Harry path, the candidates that make up the
  "tongue" sit, in 30-digit arithmetic, on the straight radial arm ahead of Harry. Their double-precision positions
  are off by a median of 0.23 (t = 150.15) and 0.46 (t = 150.2), against a gap tolerance of 0.01. A closed-loop
  simulation in 30-digit mpmath (all candidate positions, all new-candidate replays and the controller in extended
  precision) runs through t = 150 with no change in the candidate count, and also through t = 178, the end of the
  extended-precision budget (alpha_B = 3.608, unseen length 83.2, candidate count 296). Its state is converged in precision (30 vs 50 digits identical to 1e-6), in step (dt halved: Harry
  differs by 4e-6 at t = 151.5) and in resolution (tau halved: 7e-6).
  In double precision the breakdown time does not converge. Refining resolution makes it come *earlier*
  (137-150), because the round-off error of the computed front (2e-3 to 8e-3 at t = 140-148) exceeds the finer gap
  tolerances. Re-starting a double run from the exact 30-digit state at t = 151 or t = 160 breaks down again within
  8.4 or 0.5 time units. New candidates are replayed from t = 0, and that replay is hopelessly ill-conditioned by
  then. So **double precision cannot follow this sweep past t ~ 150, and extended precision can**. Whether the
  sweep completes is still open: at the measured rate (alpha_B rises about 0.017 per unit time, and the unseen
  length falls only slowly, 86.5 -> 83.2 over t = 150-178) a completed sweep would need T of about 3.4e2. That is about 3 more hours of
  30-digit simulation, which is outside this run's budget.
* **Alternative strategy at v = 0.8: the leg-greedy chase (Strategy LG of Prop. D6) fails fast.** After 17 legs
  (t ~ 16) the target starts running away: rho_target goes 0.47 -> 1.6 -> 6 -> 88 -> 1.3e4, and the remaining-arc
  length goes 26 -> 140 -> 2.7e5. Proviso (R) is also violated (11 forward-ray crossings in 3 legs). Other
  two-point parameter choices sweep at the same rate or slower and also hit double-precision breakdowns. The
  extended-precision two-point run above is the only configuration found here that gets past alpha_B = pi.
* **Task 2 (LG chase, v in {0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2}, eps from 1e-1 to 1e-5): everything is eps-independent as eps -> 0.**
  * T(v, eps) converges: for example T(0.2) = 12.78, 13.19, 13.236, 13.241 and T(0.01) = 7.197, 7.455, 7.477,
    7.479 for eps = 1e-1 ... 1e-4. Halving tau changes T by <= 2.5e-4 at eps = 1e-3 and <= 4.4e-5 at eps = 1e-4.
    The epoch-interpolation scheme agrees with exact replay from t = 0 to <= 1e-4 at eps = 1e-3.
  * The maxima of K over the chase are eps-independent to 3-4 digits:
    * the bearing total variation (TV) peaks at **about 5.1-5.2**, reached at the first switch t_eps, when the
      remaining arc wraps around Harry;
    * over the rest of the chase TV peaks at **2.90-3.04**;
    * the weighted K_w of Prop. D6 peaks at **about 3.14** at t_eps and **1.97-2.07** later.
  * **Proviso (R) held in every run:** no remaining-arc point was ever caught. The forward-ray crossing nearest to
    Harry was at >= 1.05 eps from him, while being caught would need a crossing within rho_T - eps/2 (about 0.5 eps).
  * The far end S_{2pi} = S_0 stays O(1) away during the chase, and Harry ends the sweep 1.4-5.0 eps from it.
    At v = 0.2 this end distance in eps units grows slowly as eps -> 0 (2.1 -> 4.8).
  * The eps = 1e-5 runs were stopped by the 40-minute wall clock at 60-95% of the sweep. Their K maxima, which
    occur early, agree with eps = 1e-4 to 3 digits.

## Task 1: the v = 0.8 breakdown

### 1.1 Set-up
The controller, kernel and refinement are those of `code/F/sim.py`:
* two-point controller: theta = psi_B + k (psi_C - psi_B), k = -1, c = 2;
* every Harry step is exact (the (C,h) translation);
* new candidates are midpoint labels alpha, replayed exactly from t = 0 along the recorded path;
* gap tolerance max(tau eps, eta rho).

is the first time the candidate count n exceeds 3 x its median over the previous 10 time units, or the cap of 4000
candidates is hit.

New code:
* `code/F2/mpkernel.py`: the same straight-step map in mpmath. Newton for k^{-1} starts from the double root and
  falls back to bisection; it also gives the exact minimum distance over a step. Replaying one candidate over
  15000 steps agrees to all printed digits at 30 and 50 digits (`mp_speedtest.py`).
* `v08_res.py`: double runs with a candidate cap.
* `dbl_cont.py`: double continuation of a checkpoint with a changed dt or tau.
* `v08_mpfront.py`: fixed-path double vs mp comparison.
* `mp_closed.py`: the closed loop in mpmath, started or resumed from a checkpoint.
* `dbl_from_mp.py`: a double run re-synchronised with an mp state.

### 1.5 Classification
**(a) artefact**, for the t ~ 150 breakdown (and for every other breakdown observed in this study, at 137-174,
since all of them depend on the resolution or disappear in mp). Evidence:
1. In 30-digit arithmetic the exact front for the same path has no tongue.
2. The 30/50-digit closed loop runs through t = 150 and t = 160, and is converged in dps, dt and tau.
3. The double breakdown time moves with resolution in the direction expected from a noise floor (finer tolerance
   breaks earlier).

What remains open: whether the two-point sweep at v = 0.8 *completes*. Extrapolating the measured alpha_B rate
(0.0164-0.0168 per unit time over 150-178) gives T ~ 340. The unseen length decreases slowly, by about 0.13 per unit time. In
30-digit mpmath this would need about 3 h of wall time on 10-14 processes, which was not done. It cannot be done
in double precision at all.

## Task 2: the LG chase for fixed v, eps -> 0 (`code/F2/lg.py`)

### 2.1 Method
* Strategy LG as in `paper/sec_D1.tex`:
  * leg 0 runs straight at S_0 until rho = eps/2;
  * each later leg runs straight at the first unseen direction (in alpha order) until it is at eps/2;
  * the target stays on the forward ray, so each leg is **one exact straight step**, of duration
    (rho_T - eps/2)/(1 - v), applied with the code/F kernel, including the exact seen test.
* The remaining arc R = {S_alpha : alpha_target <= alpha <= 2 pi} is kept as a polyline with gap
  <= max(tau eps, eta rho), eta = tau, over all pairs, seen or not. One seen point is kept before the target, so
  the seen/unseen boundary is located to tau eps.
* New candidates, two modes:
  * "exact": the label is alpha and the candidate is replayed from t = 0;
  * "epochs" (default): every M legs the current polyline becomes the reference. A new point is placed by cubic
    Hermite interpolation on the reference at its start-of-epoch position, then replayed **exactly** over the legs
    since the epoch began.
* Diagnostics at every leg start, over R:
  * TV: total variation of the bearing from Harry ("TV unseen" counts only segments with both ends unseen);
  * K_w = sum over segments of |r x d|^2 / (|r|^3 |d|): the weighted K of Prop. D6, and K_w <= TV;
  * Lambda: the length of R;
  * d_far = |H - S_{2pi}|, where S_{2pi} is the point S_0 (seen at t_eps);
  * the (R) test (below).

**(R) test.** During a leg the forward ray {C = 0, h > 0} is invariant. A chord of R that does not touch the target
and crosses the forward ray at distance p < rho_T - eps/2 belongs to a point that would be caught during the leg.
We count such crossings, and also record the nearest crossing.

**Late-phase quantities.** These are maxima/minima taken after Harry first gets more than 0.5 away from S_0, which
excludes the trivial start, where d_far = eps/2 at t_eps. The closest approach of R to Harry is computed exactly
over each leg, excluding the points within arclength 3 eps of the target.

Runs:
* `run_lg_grid.py`: all (v, eps) at tau = 0.1 and 0.05 with M = 100;
* M = 25 for eps in {1e-3, 1e-4};
* exact replay for eps in {1e-1, 1e-2, 1e-3}.

Outputs: `logs/lg_runs.jsonl`, time series `out/lg_ts_<tag>.npy`, tables `out/lg_tables.md` (from
`lg_analyze.py`), plots `out/lg_summary.png` and `out/lg_timeseries.png`.

* For eps = 1e-5 the late minimum is not available, since the runs were truncated before the end.
* **(R) holds in every run, at both resolutions:** zero catching crossings. Where a forward-ray crossing exists,
  it lies just beyond the target region, at >= 1.05 eps from Harry. For a point to be caught the crossing would
  have to be within rho_T - eps/2, which is about 0.5 eps. The margin is about 0.5 eps, i.e. O(eps) and not
  shrinking relative to eps.
* Apart from the target's own arc-neighbourhood, no remaining-arc point came closer to Harry than the exclusion
  radius allows (min rho / eps = 3.48-3.50, which is the value at the exclusion boundary itself).

### 2.5 Convergence checks (summary)
* **Resolution doubling** (tau 0.1 -> 0.05): T changes by <= 1.2e-2 at eps = 0.1, <= 3.7e-3 at eps = 1e-2 and
  <= 2.5e-4 at eps = 1e-3 and <= 4.4e-5 at eps = 1e-4. K maxima change by <= 0.017 at eps = 0.1 and agree to 3 digits at eps <= 1e-3. The
  t_eps value of TV changes by up to 0.04 (discussed in 2.3).
* **Epoch length** (M = 25 vs 100): T agrees to <= 4e-4.
* **Exact replay from t = 0** vs epochs: T agrees to <= 1.0e-4 (eps = 1e-3), <= 5.2e-3 (eps = 1e-2) and <= 7.9e-3
  (eps = 1e-1).
* **No precision failures** (prec_fail = 0) in any LG run. At v <= 0.2 the open-loop sensitivity exponent
  v/(1-v) is <= 0.25, so double precision is adequate here, unlike at v = 0.8.
