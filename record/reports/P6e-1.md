## 1. Why start S makes this clean (and what was wrong with C's own first contact)

With S, O is the true corner and t_0 = 1/(1−v): the arm at t_0 is exactly P6c's Claim 1 (η = 0), tan φ_0 ≤ 0.63 r with no
ε/r term, and Harry's path has no scale-ε kink: the connector direction u is within 0.72ε of u_0 = e^{iθ*} and the first leg
within 3.15ε of u (Lemma E2(b)). Then p(τ) has no c_0·ε term, and the estimate is clean down to r = 16ε (the constant 16 is only used for
ρ ≥ 0.865(r + D) in the crude far-field integrals). For C's own first contact the
defect has terms of size vε (the s_0-shift) and c_0ε (kink headings); the same proof would give an extra term
≈ C(c_0C_A + 1)ε/r in ζ·(1/v)·v, i.e. a bound only for r ≥ Mε with M ≫ c_0C_A — still lossless, but not written.

P6d's warning (tip curvature ≈ 0.46 v²/ε after the connector) is consistent and harmless here: the connector is part of
the path for τ ∈ [0, 1.4ε], the scaling centre is the corner O, and Q = ζ + (vτ/2ρ) sin 2φ with φ ≈ 2v·τ/(r+τ) gives
Q ≈ v²τ²/(r+τ)² ~ ¼v² at τ ~ r ~ ε: exactly that curvature, automatically inside the bound 0.56 v. No separate
"initial data after the connector" is needed (initial data are taken at t_0).

## 2. The heading modulus (Lemma E2) — where Theorem B is used

For τ ≤ R/400: |P'(t_0 + τ) − u_0| ≤ ϑ̄(τ) = 520M(τ + 2ε)^{1/4} ≈ 20.9 (τ + 2ε)^{1/4} (R = 10⁻³). Proof: chain (TL) at time t_i
(legs i and j inside a common family pair), (TL) at t_i and t_j for the bridging pair I' = (a_j, a_j + λ), and the rotation
of the chord I' between t_i and t_j, which is ≤ 18.4 v·θ because both endpoint velocities v r̂ make an angle ≤ 1.15θ with

*large* constant, so the window where the heading is within v/40 of u_0 is tiny: τ_3 = ½(v/(20800M))⁴ ≈ 10⁻¹² v⁴. This costs
nothing: the remaining amplification is exp(O(vC_A log(1/τ_3))) = 1 + O(vC_A log(1/v)).

## 5. How close is the arm to the exact straight-departure hook? (request of the coordinator, for the endgame / P6f)

**PROVED (Corollary E7; v ≤ 10⁻¹², 16ε ≤ r ≤ r_2, τ ≤ τ_3).** The two exact identities of the model, Q = (vτ/2ρ) sin 2φ and
r∂_rx = (x − O) − vτr̂, hold for the real arm up to
 |ζ| ≤ 0.31 r/v + 2.2 ϑ̄(τ),  |η|/(r + τ) ≤ 0.63 r (r/(r+τ))^{1−ν} + 2.01 v ϑ̄(τ),  ϑ̄(τ) = 20.9 (τ + 2ε)^{1/4},
and the bearing is within 2v(r/(r+τ))^{1−ν} + ϑ̄(τ) of −u_0. So "arm = hook + perturbation" holds with a perturbation that is
small at small scales **and small times**: C r (initial curvature of the arm, a = 1) + C·(heading deviation of Harry up to
time τ) (proved modulus τ^{1/4}; true modulus ≈ 0.9 τ). At the entry time, in the proved range of v, the arm is the
arm at τ_3 up to relative 2·10⁻⁶ (the far field does nothing for v log(1/v) C_A ≪ 1).

**NOT proved, and in the literal form FALSE for moderate v: "at the entry time the arm at scale r is the pure
straight-departure hook profile Q_m(T/r) up to C r^a".** NUMERICAL (`hook_compare.py`, `hook_v*.out`, ε = 10⁻⁴;
Q_m(λ) = model profile, sup Q_m = 0.0406, 0.0832, 0.1109 for v = 0.1, 0.2, 0.3):
 * in the window (τ = 10⁻³, 10⁻², 10⁻¹, no fit, λ = τ/r): sup|Q − Q_m(τ/r)| over r ∈ [1.6·10⁻³, 1.6·10⁻²) is
   7.2·10⁻³, 2.4·10⁻³, 4.9·10⁻⁴ (v = 0.1, 0.2, 0.3) — this is |Q_0| ≈ κ_0 r·(propagator), i.e. the proved C r term with a = 1,
   essentially independent of τ up to 0.1 (growth ≤ 30 %);
 * at the entry time, with ONE fitted effective time T_eff (0.41, 0.86, 0.29): sup|Q − Q_m(T_eff/r)| on the same r-range is
   3.0·10⁻³, 1.6·10⁻², 2.7·10⁻² — for v ≥ 0.2 this is 15–25 % of the hook size and does not improve at the smaller radii.
Reason (HEURISTIC, but each ingredient is one of the proved identities): after a time τ' ≫ r the pair (φ̄, Q) of the point
r evolves by φ̄' = (v/2ρ) sin(2φ̄ + 2e), Q' = (v/ρ) cos 2φ·Q + O(v r w/ρ²), where ρ(τ), e(τ) depend on r only through
O(r/τ). So for all small scales the same r-independent nonlinear map (determined by Harry's whole path: curved departure,
loop, return) acts on the model's one-parameter family (φ̄_m(λ), Q_m(λ)). For v ≤ 10⁻¹² this map is the identity up to
10⁻⁶; for fixed v it changes Q by a factor exp(∫(v/ρ)cos 2φ) = 1 + O(v·log(1/τ') + vT), not small as r → 0, and not a
reparametrisation λ ↦ T/r of the pure hook.
**CONJECTURE (the statement the endgame should use):** at the entry time, for r ≤ r_*, the arm is the image of the model
family under that universal map, up to a perturbation ≤ C[(r/τ')^{1−ν} + ϑ(τ')] minimised over τ', i.e. ≤ C r^{a} with
a = γ/(1+γ) for a γ-Hölder heading (a = 1/5 with the proved γ = 1/4, a = 1/2 with the true γ = 1). Equivalently:
(E_Q) plus "ζ and η small, with the bounds of Corollary E7" — these are exactly the wiggle-free statement: the proved
bound on ζ says that Q minus the hook term is ≤ 0.31 r/v + 2.2ϑ̄, with no room for scale-r wiggles of size comparable to v
unless r/v or ϑ̄ is. What is missing for P6f is (1) the comparison of φ itself with the model's φ_m(λ) (needs the
linearised φ̄-equation with the same saturated propagator; the estimate would be |φ̄ − φ̄_m| ≲ (0.2 r + 1.1ϑ̄(τ))/1, not
written), and (2) the propagation of (ζ, η)-smallness through the far field for non-tiny v.
