## 0. Headlines
1. **The bulk of W is ε-uniform in every norm measured (ε from 10⁻¹ to 10⁻⁵, v = 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2).**
   For θ ∈ [0.5, 2π − 0.5] the max walked stretch converges: 1.084, 1.224, 1.506, 2.319; fitted exponent in ε:
   0.0002 (zero). The growth "≈ ε^{−v}" of D2-1/D3-1 comes only from the two ENDS of the sweep.
2. **(FLAT_1) holds in the bulk uniformly in ε:** sin φ(s) ≤ 0.49 s for ε ≤ s ≤ 0.25. The curvature ahead of Harry is
   bounded, κ ∈ [0.37, 0.98]; it is a smooth outer profile plus a universal boundary layer in s/ε of amplitude ≈ 0.13
   decaying like (ε/s)². Plain Hölder norms of κ are NOT ε-uniform ([κ]_{1/2} ≈ 0.09 ε^{−1/2}); the scale-invariant
   modulus is. K = ∫ sin²φ/ρ ds ≤ 2.07 always; its near part (s < 0.25) is ≤ 0.0075.
3. **The heuristic laws of L-6 §2 are confirmed quantitatively**, with two corrections: constant curvature is neutral
   only to leading order (exact residual −(3/2)(v/ρ)κ sin²φ); the amplification of a tangent difference across a
   separation δ is (s0/(e·δ))^μ in total, μ = v/(1−v), reached gradually (formula in §2), not (s0/s)^μ without limit.
   Hölder loss μ for the curvature measured: 0.237 at v = 0.2 (μ = 0.25).
4. **The D2-1 failures (v = 0.25, ε = 0.005; v = 0.3, ε = 0.01) are discretisation artefacts** (uniform θ-grid too
   coarse at the tip). With the tip resolved W completes for v ≤ 0.5 at every ε tested (10⁻² … 10⁻⁴), with T
   converged in ε for v ≤ 0.4. v = 0.5 is not converged; v = 0.6 did not complete by t = 3000 (unresolved numerics).
5. **The two ends are where ε-uniformity of sup norms fails for W**: start side stretch = c·ε^{−v/(1−v)} exactly
   (created by the head-on Phase 1); left-behind tip ε^{−p(v)}, p > v/(1−v) (0.12, 0.41, 0.68, 1.04 at v = 0.1, 0.2,
   0.3, 0.4), and sup sin φ → 0.74 there. The tip is NOT a universal profile at scale ε (slow drift with log 1/ε).
   The walked LENGTH in the tip converges (1.1431 at v = 0.2 for θ > 2π − 0.5).
6. **Best design: replace the head-on Phase 1 by a tangential merge ("ramp"), keep W unchanged.** Then the max
   stretch over the WHOLE sweep is ε-independent (1.39, 1.99, 4.67 at v = 0.05, 0.1, 0.2 for ε = 10⁻²…10⁻⁵; at
   v = 0.3 it is 16.9 down to 10⁻⁴ but 33.0 at 10⁻⁵, i.e. NOT uniform there), K ≤ 2.11, K_near ≤ 0.011, T → 8.88, 10.23, 13.97. Pointwise sin φ ≤ 0.12 uniformly for v ≤ 0.1;
   at v = 0.2 it creeps up on the last windows (0.15 → 0.23 at ε = 10⁻⁵), so the end of R still has ε-scale ripples.
   Window length (λ = 0.25, 0.5) and live pursuit change nothing essential.

## 6. What a proof should aim at
1. **Split the sweep.** Bulk: everything is bounded; ends: only the start procedure matters. Prove the theorem for
   construction R (or any tangential merge), not for head-on W: for head-on W the sup stretch really is ≥ c ε^{−v/(1−v)}
   and only L¹-type bounds can hold at the two ends.
2. **Invariant for the front ahead of Harry** (window starts, ε ≤ s ≤ r0 = 0.25), measured constants for v ≤ 0.2:
   * sin φ(s) ≤ A s with A ≤ 0.49 in the bulk; near the end of R only sin φ ≤ 0.12 (v ≤ 0.1), 0.23 (v = 0.2,
     not uniform). The integrated K_near ≤ 0.011 is uniform in all runs: aim at K, not at pointwise sin φ, at the end.
   * κ = κ_out + κ_layer, |κ_layer(s)| ≤ 0.15 min(1, (ε/s)²), κ_out ∈ [0.37, 0.98] varying on scale 1.
     Equivalent scale-invariant form: |κ(s+δ) − κ(s)| ≤ N₁ δ/s for ε ≤ δ ≤ s, N₁ ≈ 0.1 (0.12 at v = 0.2).
     Do NOT use an unweighted Hölder norm of κ: it is ≈ 0.09 ε^{−1/2}.
   * K ≤ 2.11 (R) / 2.07 (W) at all times; stretch ≤ 1.39, 1.99, 4.67 (v = 0.05, 0.1, 0.2).
3. **Why the layer is harmless**: its contribution to sin φ at distance s is ≲ ε·(layer amplitude) and
   ∫ sin²φ/ρ over it is O(10⁻³); it is regenerated identically at each window (universal in s/ε), it does not
   accumulate. The (ε/s)² decay matches L-6's estimate for an O(ε) offset of Harry from the front.
4. **Amplification budget**: a tangent difference at separation δ grows by at most (s₀/(eδ))^μ, a curvature
   difference by ≈ 1.45 (s₀/(eδ))^μ; κ of a circle through Harry drifts by −(3/16) μ κ³ per unit approach. The initial
   front after a ramp is smooth at scale 1 away from the merge point, so only the O(1)-scale amplification acts in
   the bulk — consistent with the measured bounded κ_out.
5. **Open on the numerical side**: ε = 10⁻⁶ for construction R at v = 0.1, 0.2 and a resolution check of the v = 0.3, ε = 10⁻⁵ break; an extended-precision check; the Lipschitz
   constant of κ_out; v ≥ 0.3 for R; whether a smoother end of the ramp (e.g. stopping rule or α law) removes the
   ε-scale ripples in the tail; v ∈ [0.4, 0.6] for head-on W.
